# Care Task Escalation

Purpose: Explains how a nurse care task moves through its statuses and when a nurse should raise a problem with a doctor.

## Care task statuses

A nurse care task in Clinical Staff Management starts as pending, then becomes acknowledged, and then becomes completed. The nurse acknowledges the task to show that it has been seen. The nurse marks the task completed once the care has been done.

## Cancelling a care task

A nurse care task can be cancelled when it is no longer needed, for example when it was entered by mistake. The nurse should give a short reason when cancelling a care task. A completed care task cannot be cancelled, and a cancelled care task is not reopened.

## Completing a care task after discharge

A nurse care task can still be completed after the patient has been discharged. This lets a nurse close out a task that was still open at the time of discharge. The completed care task stays on the record of its admission, so the record shows how the task ended.

## When a pending care task is overdue

A pending nurse care task that has not been acknowledged within 60 minutes is considered overdue. The nurse who notices an overdue care task should tell the nurse in charge. If the delay could affect the patient's plan, the nurse should also tell the assigned doctor.

## Raising a care task concern with a doctor

When a nurse cannot carry out a care task safely, or sees something that worries them, the nurse should tell the assigned doctor. The doctor decides whether to change the plan or to raise a consultation request so that a specialist can advise. The application does not make this decision for the nurse or the doctor.

## AI suggestions about care tasks

Any AI output about nurse care tasks is advisory only. A doctor, nurse or specialist reviews it and makes every decision. AI does not acknowledge, complete or cancel a care task.
