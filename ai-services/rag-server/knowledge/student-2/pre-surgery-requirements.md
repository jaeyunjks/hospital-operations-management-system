# Pre-Surgery Requirements

Purpose: Explains what must be true before a surgery can be scheduled and what happens once it is scheduled.

## Who can schedule a surgery

Only the assigned doctor for the admission can schedule a surgery. A nurse or a specialist cannot schedule a surgery, even if a specialist recommends one. A doctor who is not assigned to the admission also cannot schedule it.

## The admission must be active to schedule a surgery

A surgery can only be scheduled while the admission is active. If the patient has been discharged, or the admission is no longer active for any other reason, the surgery cannot be scheduled. Surgery for a patient who is back in hospital needs an active admission.

## Surgery date and time

A surgery must be scheduled for a date and time in the future. A surgery scheduled for a time that has already passed is not accepted. The assigned doctor enters the date and time when scheduling.

## Theatre request to Room and Bed Management

When a surgery is scheduled, the application sends a theatre request to Room and Bed Management. Room and Bed Management is a separate service that looks after theatre space. Sending the theatre request does not by itself guarantee a theatre slot.

## Preparing before a surgery

Before scheduling a surgery, the assigned doctor may raise a consultation request so that a specialist can give a recommendation. A consultation request is optional before surgery. Nurse care tasks that prepare the patient should be completed before the scheduled surgery time.

## AI suggestions about surgery

Any AI output about a surgery is advisory only. A doctor, nurse or specialist reviews it and makes every decision. AI does not schedule a surgery or send a theatre request.
